# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: a Czech film crew member opening a personal link on a phone, often the night before or around 5 am, in poor light and possibly without signal. They need to know when, where, what changed for them, and to say they know.

Secondary: the 1st AD or production coordinator who publishes changes and chases whoever has not confirmed. Buyers are producers and heads of production; which of them pays is not yet known.

## Product Purpose

FilmRunner is a living call sheet. When the plan changes, everyone finds out, and the AD knows that they did. Success is a real shoot where nobody turns up at the old place or time.

## Positioning

A call sheet that shows each person their own change, asks them to confirm it, and tells the AD who has not. It is honest about being offline. It does not decide what to do when a plan changes and does not calculate cost.

## Operating Context

Film set, unit base, van, night before a shoot. Cold hands, one hand, low light, poor signal. The incumbent is a PDF in email or WhatsApp. Revisions are named by letter and colour on set: B blue, C pink, D yellow, E green, F goldenrod, G buff, H salmon, I cherry. Vocabulary: sraz, obraz, dispozice, vyzvednutí, na place, maska.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React and Vite PWA on GitHub Pages, FastAPI on Fly, Postgres on Supabase. Crew use a link with no account or install. Sheets print to paper and must stay legible in print. Czech and English interface; user-entered text is never translated. Cost impact, availability checks and automatic SMS are not built.

## Brand Commitments

Name: FilmRunner. Revision colours and the large personal call time stay. The founder rejected the previous cream-paper look on 2026-11-05 as template-like, empty and without character; nothing else about it is binding.

## Evidence on Hand

Cold outreach named a sudden plan change as the biggest pain; the exact replies are not in the repo. No real crew has used the product yet. No customers, testimonials or benchmarks exist, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The person's own time and place come first; everything else waits.
2. Say what is true, including when it is out of date or unconfirmed.
3. It has to work one-handed, half asleep and with no signal.
4. The AD's next action is obvious, not one choice among many.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast and 44px touch targets, in low light. Colour never carries meaning alone: the revision letter always accompanies its colour.
